I led a team of 25 students in designing, manufacturing, and building an off-road vehicle to compete in the BAJA SAE India competition, where teams design and build an off-road vehicle to compete in events such as acceleration, suspension, traction, and a final 4-hour endurance race, all using the same engine to emphasize design optimization over raw power.

As captain and technical head of powertrain systems, I was responsible for leading the design, manufacturing, and testing of the vehicle's drivetrain, as well as managing and mentoring the rest of the team to ensure deadlines were met without sacrificing reliability.

## Custom Powertrain System

I led the development of a custom powertrain system to reduce vehicle weight and gain a performance advantage over teams using bulkier, non-tunable OEM systems. This included a custom 2-stage compound gearbox, which required machining our own aluminum casing and reduced gearbox weight by roughly 2.5 kg (35%) while maintaining the required torque and an efficient cooling solution for the 4-hour endurance race.

<img src="http://cshah96.github.io/ChinmayShah.github.io/images/powertrain.png" alt="Alt Text" width="600" height="500" align="center">

![Distance to line](http://cshah96.github.io/ChinmayShah.github.io/images/gearbox2.png)

We also designed a custom composite velocity (CV) joint with a 2mm thin steel insert to handle frictional wear that a pure aluminum joint couldn't withstand, reducing CV joint weight by about 40% (1.5 kg each) using non-conventional manufacturing processes like wire cutting and EDM. This earned the team a runner-up award in the technological innovation category.

![Distance to line](http://cshah96.github.io/ChinmayShah.github.io/images/CVJ.png)

## Continuously Variable Transmission (CVT)

To improve acceleration without sacrificing top speed, I helped design and manufacture a custom tunable CVT — a centrifugal clutch system with a V-belt driven pulley setup that smoothly transitions between high and low gear ratios depending on the event.

The custom CVT weighed 1.5 kg (25%) less than the OEM unit it replaced, improved acceleration by about 0.25 seconds, and improved top speed by around 10% in another tuning configuration. We also built a custom electronic tuning system to adjust the CVT for peak performance across different events, placing among the top four teams (out of 160) to develop a custom CVT system.
