Building on my previous work in human motion forecasting, I am currently extending the framework to directly control a hip-knee exoskeleton. This involves separating the intent-recognition and motion-prediction stage from a dedicated robot control layer, and researching control strategies that leverage the predicted motion intent to maximize biomechanical benefit for the user. This work is in progress.
