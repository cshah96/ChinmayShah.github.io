A phase-conditioned deep learning architecture for real-time prediction of lower-limb kinematics across subjects and locomotion modes, enabling anticipatory control of wearable robotic exoskeletons.

**Authors:** C. Shah, J. Li, and G. Clark

**Venue:** Submitted to IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS), Pittsburgh, 2026 — *under review*

A Periodic Autoencoder (DeepPhase-inspired) is trained on the Georgia Tech Lower-Limb Dataset to learn a compact, frequency-domain phase representation of human gait. The encoder-decoder architecture predicts future joint angles for the hip, knee, and ankle across unseen subjects and locomotion modes (walking, running, stair ascent/descent, ramp). Evaluation on held-out subjects achieves a mean absolute error of 4–8° and R² greater than 0.75. The learned phase representation serves as the foundation for the FLD encoder pipeline driving anticipatory control of a hip-knee exoskeleton.
