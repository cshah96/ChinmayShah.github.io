An EMG-driven musculoskeletal model controller that restores volitional ankle torque generation in a robotic prosthesis by replicating the biological neuromuscular pathway through a Hill-type muscle model.

**Authors:** C. Shah, A. Fleming, V. Nalam, M. Liu, and H. H. Huang

**Venue:** IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS), Kyoto, 2022

The controller maps surface EMG signals from residual limb muscles through a 2-muscle Hill-type musculoskeletal model to estimate volitional ankle joint torques in real time. Subject-specific model parameters are identified via non-linear optimization against inverse dynamics ground truth, achieving greater than 85% torque prediction accuracy. This approach preserves continuous, intuitive volitional control — aligning prosthetic assistance with the user's neural intent rather than switching between discrete locomotion modes.
